## Part 1: Trades

### What the engine really does (plain English)

There are four trade tools on the Trades screen, in this order:

1. **Trade ideas** (`/api/trades/smart`, `core/trades/bilateral.ts`). The app
   prices every rostered player on **this week's** number only (the betting
   line when complete, else Sleeper's published week). It builds 1-for-1,
   2-for-1 and 1-for-2 packages against every other roster, runs the lineup
   solver on both rosters **for this week**, and keeps a package when:
   your lineup gains at least **1.0 point this week**; the two sides' values
   (summed) are within 25% of each other; the other team's lineup loses no
   more than **0.75 points this week**; and the other team gains points or has
   a roster "reason" (for example "moves surplus without opening a slot").
   Up to 5 ideas are shown. Each is then run through Check a trade (rest of
   season) and dropped only if that says it favors the other team.
2. **Buy low / sell high**, the same board's second section: players whose
   production this season is 1.5+ points a game off their preseason
   projection.
3. **Build a trade**: Alex marks who he would sell and want; packages are
   scored over the rest of the season with Check a trade's model.
4. **Check a trade**: any deal, both sides' best lineups week by week to week
   17, byes and injuries counted. A player's weekly rate is this week's number
   carried forward to every remaining week.

Plus a folded **Explore the market** list: Trade target / Sell high / Trade
away labels from newsletter mention counts.

## Part 2: Start/Sit and lineup

### What the engine really does (plain English)

- **The number.** Betting lines turned into points (yards times the league
  rate, catches times 0.5, anytime-TD chance times 6). Where the lines are not
  complete, Sleeper's published week, ranked 2 points lower (the settled
  minus-2 rule). Small nudges (news, matchup, usage) capped at 10% of the base.
  Questionable counts in full with a chip (settled). Doubtful is a flat -6.
  Out, IR, PUP and suspended are removed. Byes have no number.
- **The lineup.** An exact solver over the league's real slots, so a QB can
  only ever go in the QB slot. Team shows the swaps from Sleeper's current
  lineup worth 0.75 points or more.
- **Best move on Team** has two kinds: "Start A over B" (a swap within the
  roster) and, since 18 October, "Pick up and start A over B" (a free agent who
  beats the best lineup Alex can already make by 0.5+).
- **Floor / Ceiling.** There is no toggle any more. The server picks the mode
  from the projected margin: favorite by 10+ is Floor, underdog by 10+ is
  Ceiling, else Balanced (`core/startsit/modeSuggest.ts:64`). Waivers, Trades
  and Matchup always use Balanced. What a mode changes: Floor multiplies the
  Vegas number by 0.9 and status and weather by 1.3 and 1.2; Ceiling multiplies
  Vegas by 1.2 and weather by 0.9 (`core/startsit/mode.ts:55-101`). The usage,
  role, TD and explosiveness parts it also weights are display-only today
  (`core/startsit/scoringRules.ts:69`).

### The QB-over-WR case: cause found

**A bug in the "Pick up and start" headline, not in the slot rules.** The
lineup itself is legal: the free-agent QB goes into the QB slot. The headline's
"over" player is worked out as "anyone in Sleeper's current lineup who is not in
the new best lineup, lowest number first" (`core/waivers/pickupStart.ts:345`,
`overOf`). That is a plain difference of two lineups, not the player the free
agent replaces. When Alex's Sleeper lineup has any other weaker starter, even
one below Team's own 0.75 swap bar, that player is named instead of the QB the
free agent actually replaces.

Reproduced with the real function on Alex's week 7 roster (the fixture in
`tests/waivers.pickupStart.test.ts`) plus a free-agent QB at 21.5 against
Jordan Love's 20.4, while Sleeper started Emeka Egbuka (6.7) over Ladd
McConkey (7.6):

```
start: StreamQB (QB) at slot QB, gain +1.10
over:      Egbuka (WR) 6.7      <- what the headline names
displaces: Love (QB) 20.4       <- who he really replaces
Headline: "Pick up and start StreamQB over Egbuka"   21.5 vs 6.7 projected
```

The same shape comes out three other ways: a 0.5-point WR gap Team hides
(still "over Egbuka"), a WR ruled Out but still in Sleeper's lineup ("over
Collins"), and Alex's only QB on a bye with the QB slot empty ("over Egbuka").
Tapping the card opens Compare at the QB slot with a WR and a QB, which is not
comparable, so the sheet shows no pick under a headline that made one. The
correct card is "Pick up and start StreamQB over Jordan Love, +1.1"; and at
+1.1 (a 55% call) it should not be a Best move at all (X1). Shipped in #382
(18 October); the tests only cover same-position cases. Finding S1.

## Part 3: Waivers

### What the engine really does (plain English)

- **Who is looked at.** The top 12 free agents per position by preseason draft
  rank, plus the top 4 by this week's Sleeper projection, plus recent drops
  (`core/roster/freeAgents.ts:24,117`). Sleeper's trending adds are not scanned.
- **Value.** A 3-week window (this week and the next two, weighted 1, 1, 0.5
  while this week's games are ahead). This week is the Start/Sit number; the
  next weeks are Sleeper's projection for those weeks. Nothing after week 11
  counts today, including the playoffs.
- **Tiers.** "Do this" at a weighted gain of 4+, "Worth considering" at 1.5+,
  "Watch" at 0.4+. Reasons: starts now, bye or injury hole, bye depth, injury
  backup, IR stash (an Out or IR free agent 3+ points over replacement, with an
  open IR slot).
- **Drops.** The lowest drop score among options within 1 weighted point of
  the best gain. Drop score: 0.25 season average, 0.35 recent form, 0.40 the
  window projection, plus small role, tenure and handcuff terms. Protected:
  this week's lineup, rounds 1 to 6, handcuffs to elite backs, top-10 Sleeper
  adds.
- **Bids.** The smallest bid that wins about 3 times in 4 for "Do this", 1 in
  2 for "Worth considering", $0 to $1 for a stash, against rival bidders
  modelled from this season's real claims. Cap: all the money left. Value
  counted only through week 14 (`core/league/planning.ts:182`).
- **Mode.** Always Balanced.

### Against a good fantasy tool

| Pickup type Alex wants | In the app |
|---|---|
| Immediate starter | Yes ("starts now", 0.5+ this week) |
| Bye or injury cover | Yes, inside the 3-week window only |
| Upside stash | Only an injured player for an open IR slot; no healthy upside stash (handcuffs, rising roles) |
| Block a rival's need | No. Rival need is read only to price bids |
| FAAB: spend on real starters, keep money for the playoffs | No playoff reserve; playoff weeks count for nothing |
